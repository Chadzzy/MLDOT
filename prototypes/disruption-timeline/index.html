The compressing window

One visual element carries the whole emotional arc: the gap between arrival and departure, shrinking live. When it crosses the 30-minute minimum it changes state — and only then do options appear. Anxiety made visible is anxiety made manageable.

Confidence, not just fact

"78% likely to hold, from your inbound's current position." Treating the passenger as capable of handling probability is itself a premium signal. Grounded in the notebook's predict_flight_disruption() and its 0.70 risk threshold.

Live Activity as the experience

She never opens the app. The Dynamic Island carries status; the Live Activity expands only when a decision is genuinely hers to make — one moment, two buttons. Everything else is ambient.

The ledger answers the trust gap

14:20 delay detected · 15:45 seat held · 16:03 bag rerouted. A running, timestamped record of what was done on her behalf. Proof of care as an artefact — nobody builds this.

Four studies · deliberately opposite instincts

Same disruption, four bets on what care looks like

Each of these is a testable hypothesis, not a decoration. The single sentence bets on radical reduction; the pre-landing card bets on completeness; the mode shift bets on tone; the heartbeat bets on presence without interruption.
